Video File:      dataset\original_video.mp4
Resolution:      480x854
Frame Count:     242 frames @ 30.00 FPS

Technique:       Frame Differencing
Identifies:      Instantaneous pixel intensity changes between adjacent frames (t and t-1)
Strengths:       Extremely fast; zero memory requirement; immediately detects sudden fast movements.
Weaknesses:      Produces ghosting/aperture artifacts; creates hollow boundaries for uniformly colored objects; fails when objects pause.
Observations:    Detected an average of 28438 changing pixels per frame during vehicle and pedestrian motion.

Technique:       GMM Background Subtraction (MOG2)
Identifies:      Persistent foreground objects distinct from a statistical Gaussian mixture background model
Strengths:       Segments complete solid object silhouettes; robust to gradual illumination changes; detects shadows separately.
Weaknesses:      Requires warm-up training period; susceptible to background camera jitter / panning.
Observations:    Extracted solid vehicle and pedestrian shapes with an average of 64596 foreground pixels.
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Technique:       Lucas–Kanade Optical Flow
Identifies:      2D velocity vectors (dx/dt, dy/dt) and motion trajectories of salient Shi-Tomasi feature points
Strengths:       Provides exact directional velocity and trajectory information for individual vehicles/pedestrians.
Weaknesses:      Sparse tracking only; assumes brightness constancy and small displacements; fails on textureless surfaces.
Observations:    Tracked up to 120 concurrent feature points across traffic trajectories.
