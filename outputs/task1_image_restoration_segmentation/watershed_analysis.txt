TASK 1B: WATERSHED SEGMENTATION PERFORMANCE & ANALYSIS
============================================================

Dataset Nature: Complex outdoor urban landscape with sky, clouds, rainbow, buildings, and trees.

Observations on Watershed Results:
1. Over-segmentation tendency:
   - The watershed algorithm treats pixel intensity as a topographic surface.
   - In this photograph, subtle cloud gradients, atmospheric rainbow dispersion, and
     tree texture produce numerous local minima.
   - Consequently, watershed creates multiple subdivided basins in the sky and foliage,
     leading to characteristic over-segmentation rather than clean single-object boundaries.

2. Building Horizon vs Sky separation:
   - The sharp contrast between building silhouettes and the sky is effectively identified
     as major catchment divides.

3. Practical Engineering Implication:
   - Watershed operates best on high-contrast, well-isolated objects (such as biomedical cells
     or coins on a flat backdrop) where Euclidean distance transform provides distinct centers.
   - For complex outdoor natural scenes, semantic deep learning segmentation (e.g. Mask R-CNN / YOLO-Seg)
     is far superior to purely intensity/gradient-driven morphological watershed.
